06 // TECHNICAL ARSENAL & SHOP CAPABILITIES

Technical Skills

CAD packages, machine-shop equipment, rapid prototyping tools, and embedded test hardware applied across propulsion, mechanisms, and mechatronics projects.

CAD & Analysis

  • SolidWorks (3D part modeling, large assemblies, GD&T drawings, CSWA prep)
  • Onshape (cloud collaborative assemblies & recovery bay packaging)
  • Creo Parametric & Autodesk Fusion 360
  • MATLAB & OpenRocket (flight trajectory & data reduction)
  • Design for Manufacturing & Assembly (DFMA)
  • Tolerance stack-up & iterative prototyping

CNC & Manual Machining

  • Manual Precision Lathe (Hardinge — facing, turning, shoulders, collet stops)
  • Manual Vertical Mill (edge-finder setups, V-block fixturing, boring, broaching)
  • CNC Mill & Lathe Operations (Tormach & Haas setups)
  • SolidWorks CAM, G-code & M-code fundamentals
  • Aluminum (6061-T6), brass, steel, Delrin & phenolic stock
  • Precision metrology (calipers, micrometers, dial indicators, DROs)

Rapid Prototyping & Shop

  • FDM 3D Printing (PLA, PETG, ABS, carbon-fiber composites) & SLA resin
  • CO₂ Laser Cutting & DXF nesting (acrylic, wood, neoprene, cardboard)
  • Composite wet layup & structural epoxy bonding (high-power rocketry)
  • Sheet-metal cutting, bending, arbor-press bearing seating & hand tapping

Electronics & Instrumentation

  • Analog & digital circuit breadboarding and schematic verification
  • Through-hole & surface-mount PCB soldering and harness dressing
  • Multimeter, bench power supply & function generator diagnostics
  • Infrared thermography calibration (emissivity compensation & SOPs)
  • Static thrust-stand & voltage/current power characterization

Embedded Controls & Actuators

  • Arduino (Uno, Nano) & ESP32 firmware in C/C++
  • Raspberry Pi flight-computer & sensor-logging architectures
  • Ultrasonic, infrared, photoresistor, barometric & accelerometer sensors
  • Brushless motors/ESCs, NEMA 17 steppers, servos & DC gearmotors
  • LiPo battery safety, XT60 power distribution & RC transmitter binding

Engineering Workflow

  • Hardware root-cause failure analysis & teardown documentation
  • Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) & range safety checklists
  • Trade studies & subsystem interface control
  • Small-batch shop assembly-line setup & quality inspection
