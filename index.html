About me

When I was 14, I went to the Kennedy Space Center with my family. Standing under a full-size Saturn V is what got me. I still remember how small I felt next to it. I decided right then that I wanted to build machines like that, and lately the goal has grown: I want to pursue graduate school in astronautical engineering and help build something that flies to Mars.

My path to aerospace started in the machine shop at the Dos Pueblos Engineering Academy, continued through Santa Barbara City College, and brought me to Cal Poly Pomona, where I'm a junior focused on CAD, design for manufacturing, and building propulsion hardware with THRUST.

When something breaks, I take it apart, find the root cause, machine the fix, and test it on real hardware. And when I hit a goal, I set the next one.
