About me

When I was 14, I went to the Kennedy Space Center with my parents and my little sister. Standing under a full-size Saturn V is what got me. I still remember how small I felt next to it. I decided right then that I wanted to build machines like that, and lately the goal has grown: I want to help build something that flies to Mars.

I started in the machine shop at the Dos Pueblos Engineering Academy, finished six associate degrees at Santa Barbara City College, and transferred to Cal Poly Pomona. Now I'm an engineering intern at Raytheon, where I design and prototype tooling for semiconductor fabrication.

When something breaks, I try to fix it. If that fails, I take it apart and work through it one piece at a time. And when I hit a goal, I set the next one.
