About me

When I was 14, I went to the Kennedy Space Center with my family. Standing under a full-size Saturn V is what got me. I still remember how small I felt next to it. I decided right then that I wanted to build machines like that, and lately the goal has grown: I want to help build something that flies to Mars.

I started in the machine shop at the Dos Pueblos Engineering Academy, finished six associate degrees at Santa Barbara City College, and transferred to Cal Poly Pomona. Now I'm a junior there, and I'm on the propulsion sub-team of THRUST.

When something breaks, I try to fix it. If that fails, I take it apart and work through it one piece at a time. And when I hit a goal, I set the next one.
